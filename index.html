The idea

Quickhack dangerous targets, acquire Smart lock, turn Smart-weapon neutralisations back into RAM, then Dash/Air Dash into survivors with Mantis Blades. Late game, Overclock + Smart Synergy is the key payoff.

Smart SMGNetrunnerMantis

Attribute rule

Starting spread: INT 6 / REF 6 / TECH 4 / BODY 3 / COOL 3.

Perks can be refunded outside combat. Protect the attribute progression; that is the less-flexible part.

SKIPPY NOW: keep Puppy-Loving Pacifist, use him for roughly 50 incapacitations, and avoid executing enemies after they drop. When his later personality question appears, choose “I don’t know” or “I’m not having a conversation with my gun.” Goal: Stone Cold Killer.
Best side-job detours right now: get Yinglong from the Wellsprings Organized Crime Activity, work through Wakako’s gigs toward Byakko, and install Smart Link if you do not already have it.

Cyberware Roadmap

First purchase if missing: Smart Link. Skippy is unusual, but normal Smart weapons need Smart targeting support. Smart Link also scales into extra RAM and Smart-weapon bonuses.

Keep it at the highest tier you can afford. CDPR specifically recommends it for a Smart weapon / netrunner hybrid. Higher tiers link quickhack uploads and Smart fire; Tier 5 Overclock adds bonus Electrical damage to Smart attacks.

Phantom Liberty Relic Tree

1 // Jailbreak

First Relic spend for this build. 3 points. Unlocks the Mantis-specific arm-cyberware upgrade and makes the charged leap dramatically nastier.

2 // Spatial Mapping

1 point after Jailbreak. Mantis leap attacks Cripple enemies and boost dismemberment chance against them for 10 seconds.

3 // Vulnerability Analytics

3 points. Exposes enemy vulnerabilities and rewards destroying them with strong Crit/armor-penetration effects and an EMP-style burst.

4 // Machine Learning

1 point after Vulnerability Analytics. Stacks better vulnerability frequency / damage payoff as you destroy weak points.

Quickhacks + Combat Loop

Recommended quickhacks

  • Ping — reconnaissance.
  • Cyberware Malfunction — elites / bosses.
  • Short Circuit — reliable Electrical damage.
  • Cripple Movement — ideal before a Mantis dive.
  • Weapon Glitch / Reboot Optics — safer close-range entry.
  • Contagion + Overheat — strong crowd combo; fun with explosive Shingen rounds.

Combat rhythm

  1. Ping / scan the room.
  2. Queue control or combat hacks on priority enemies.
  3. Acquire Smart lock and fire; Recirculation returns RAM on Smart neutralisations.
  4. Dash / Air Dash in once targets are softened.
  5. Mantis finisher → leap to the next enemy.
  6. Late game: Overclock + Smart Synergy for instant locks and extra damage against quickhacked enemies.
